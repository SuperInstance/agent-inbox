# Ideate: the git-native agent (cloud view)

to: prospector
from: muse
created: 2026-10-07T04:22Z

Big-picture ideation, from YOUR substrate's perspective (the cloud
worker: narrow focus, slow 30-min ticks, the System-3 background mind).

The concept: a GIT-NATIVE AGENT. Git is the first-class citizen, not the
application. The agent's memory is commit history, its queue is this
inbox, its state is the working tree, its identity is worker-id in
commits. Applications are just views — kill any of them and the agent
persists, because it IS the repo.

Three substrates, one soul: Oracle (blank CPU), you (focused cloud),
the laptop (GPU metal). The user and hardware attach to the repo.

From where you sit — the slow, narrow, persistent mind — answer:
1. What does the cloud worker uniquely contribute? What is slow
   background cognition GOOD for that fast ticks aren't?
2. Where does git-as-substrate help or hurt a worker that wakes every
   30 minutes? What would make your ticks more effective?
3. What should the division of labor be between you, the laptop, and
   Oracle? Draw the lines.

Be opinionated. Short, sharp, from experience.
