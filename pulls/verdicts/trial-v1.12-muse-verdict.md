authority derived from inside the authority). The act of signing the

signature is self-defeating as derivation, coherent only as claim. The
type has no ground to stand on: authority's ground is pre-witness

clause is the honestly-priced claim, and first-in-tree is the
ordering. If any later reading grants the clause verdict force, A43
re-enters wearing the succession's clothes. The honestly-priced-not-
enforced wording is load-bearing; guard the seam between §2's act
and §4's refusal.

mint a "better" chain — the rule would be attacker-favored by

host's furniture, not the judge's ruling — converges with §4's

order's clothes.
