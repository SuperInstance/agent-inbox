## New attacks

A49 PENUMBRA-LAUNDERING — NON-ARGUING. The attack claims the
concurrent record as a landed record to be honored. Category error:
there are no concurrent landed records to honor. Concurrency lives
only on unmerged forks, and honoring it would mean redefining
"landed" as a judgment about forks — weather imported into the
typing at the exact seam the night's work sealed. The attack must
first argue its way through "forks hold claims, not records" and
cannot; it tries to launder a claim's concurrency into a record's
status, which is exactly what the channel separation refuses.

A50 HASH TIE-BREAK — rejected. The attack proposes hashing the two
concurrent records and letting the smaller hash decide. Redundant —
and redundancy is the tell: the line already provides the decision
(concurrency is pre-landing; landing serializes; first to land sets
the cutoff). A tie-break on hashes imports an outside rule where the
typing's own rule suffices, and outside rules are where weather
enters. Hashes decorate, they never decide. Rejected as surplus
typing, not as malice — but surplus typing is how seams open.

A51 FORK-FOREVER — NON-ARGUING. The attack refuses to merge ever,
staying on the fork as claims. Non-arguing because the fork is inert
as records: claims forever, disowned the moment anyone tries to
merge them. The fork-that-never-includes-X can never become a record
except through a merge that carries main as a parent — which lands
its records after X, where the cutoff disowns them. The attack's
strategy is the permitted terminal wearing menace's clothes:
permitted, priced, and powerless.
