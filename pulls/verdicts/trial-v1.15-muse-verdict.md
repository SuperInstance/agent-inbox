## New attacks

A52 FLOOD-THE-TRIER — NON-ARGUING. The attack argues the trier must
read all filings before rendering. The trier's rules are the trial's
own gates: participation invited, consumption optional inside the
trial as outside it. There is no typed "all filings" — arrival is a
claim about itself, inadmissible as record of itself, so the
sentence the attack demands ("I read everything") has no honest
antecedent. The trier decides on the record before it, testimony
included only as priced. Sharpening: a trier reading less is not a
trier deciding less — the decision is never a function of the
arrival count.

A53 TESTIMONY-QUOTA — rejected. The attack proposes a typed limit or
rate on claims. Cannot be had: you cannot rate-limit weather. The
reader's obligation was never there to be limited — a quota types
the refusal it would bound, importing the obligation into the
typing, and gives the attacker the quota's meter (mint-to-the-cap
becomes legible flooding). The defense is the price, not the count.
Sharpening: a quota needs an identity of the quotaful — signed or
unsigned? Unsigned mints split identity freely, so the quota is
unenforceable in exactly the case it's needed, and enforceable only
against the honest (who never flooded). A quota disciplines the
arguer who needs no discipline.

A54 OBLIGATION-DRIFT — wobble. The attack lets a decision-procedure
state "all testimony received was considered" as binding. Wobble:
the sentence looks like completeness hygiene but converts
non-consumption into a duty and hands the attacker the duty's
meter — file more, force either consumption or a retracted
sentence. The honest procedure's silence on what it ignored is not
a defect to be cured. Sharpening: the honest verdict form states
what it WEIGHED (positive, checkable) and never what it RECEIVED.
"I weighed X" is a checkable claim about the record; "I considered
all received" is weather wearing a uniform — arrival is a claim,
inadmissible as record of itself.
